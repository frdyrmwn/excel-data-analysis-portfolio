SUPPLY CHAIN ORDER FULFILLMENT & DELAY RISK DIAGNOSTIC ANALYSIS

Overview:
This project delivers an operational supply chain data analysis solution to evaluate order fulfillment risks, delivery delays, and logistical bottlenecks. By processing raw supply chain datasets, this project builds a structured Excel workbook featuring automated data preparation, pivot table aggregation, and an interactive executive supply chain dashboard to analyze shipping methods, weather impacts, supplier reliability scores, and high risk order categories.

Analyst: Muhammad Ferdy Irmawan, S.Kom.
Dataset Source: Kaggle
Primary Tool: Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning, Formulas, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Timelines, KPI Cards)

EXCEL WORKBOOK STRUCTURE AND DETAILED FUNCTIONS
The project file contains multiple sequential worksheets designed to transform raw data into actionable executive insights:
1. Studi Case: Defines the analytical framework, business background, specific case questions, and expected business outputs for the portfolio project.
2. Dataset Mentah: Stores the original raw extraction of supply chain order records containing order IDs, shipping dates, supplier reliability scores, shipping methods, weather conditions, processing times, and delay statuses.
3. Data Preparation: Performs data transformation, cleaning, and calculated field enrichments to prepare structured tables for analytical modeling.
4. Pivot: Houses multi dimensional Pivot Tables that aggregate order volumes by shipping method and priority, evaluate risk categories across weather conditions, and analyze supplier reliability versus shipping distances.
5. Dashboard: The core executive presentation sheet. It integrates dynamic Pivot Charts, interactive Slicers (shipping method, weather condition), an interactive Timeline (order date), automated KPI summary cards driven by formulas, and analytical text boxes for final conclusions.

RESEARCH QUESTIONS ADDRESSED
1. Shipping Method & Priority Distribution: How is the delivery delay rate distributed across shipping methods and order priorities?
2. Weather & Processing Time Impact: How significantly do extreme weather conditions and shipping distances impact order processing times and risk categories?
3. Supplier Reliability Correlation: Does the supplier reliability score correlate directly with ontime order fulfillment failures?
4. High Risk Route Probability: Which routes or variable combinations have the highest probability of delay risk?

KEY FINDINGS AND BUSINESS INSIGHTS
1. Shipping & Priority Distribution: Sea and Road shipping methods dominate total operational volume, while Sea routes exhibit the highest vulnerability to fulfillment delays. High priority orders require strict monitoring as they directly impact customer satisfaction.
2. Weather & Risk Impact: Extreme weather conditions such as storms dominate the High Risk order category and severely bottleneck average processing times.
3. Supplier Reliability Correlation: Delayed order statuses correlate with variations in supplier reliability scores, where underperforming suppliers contribute significantly to upstream supply chain bottlenecks.

STRATEGIC BUSINESS OUTPUT AND RECOMMENDATIONS

Route Optimization & Weather Mitigation: Implement an early warning system integrated with real-time weather data to dynamically reroute shipments when extreme weather conditions are detected.

Supplier Management & SLA Audits: Conduct regular performance audits and contract evaluations for low-reliability suppliers to mitigate upstream bottlenecks.

Dynamic Order Scheduling: Prioritize warehouse processing schedules for high-risk orders to avoid delivery penalties and maintain service level agreements.
