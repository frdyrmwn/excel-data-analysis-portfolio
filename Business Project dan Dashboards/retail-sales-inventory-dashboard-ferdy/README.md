RETAIL SUPPLY CHAIN & PRICING OPTIMIZATION DIAGNOSTIC ANALYSIS

Overview:
This project delivers an interactive executive retail dashboard and diagnostic analytics solution to solve supply chain imbalances and pricing inefficiencies. By processing raw transaction records, this project builds a structured Excel workbook featuring automated data preparation, multi dimensional pivot table aggregations, dynamic KPI summary cards, and visual analytics to evaluate product category performance, promotion effectiveness, regional demand levels, and weather driven pricing dynamics.

Analyst: Muhammad Ferdy Irmawan, S.Kom.
Dataset Source: Kaggle (Retail Inventory & Sales Dataset)
Primary Tool: Microsoft Excel (Data Preparation, Formulas, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, KPI Cards)

EXCEL WORKBOOK STRUCTURE AND DETAILED FUNCTIONS
1. The project file contains multiple sequential worksheets designed to transform raw data into actionable executive insights:

2. Task: Defines the business background, analytical framework, specific diagnostic case questions, and strategic operational outputs for the retail supply chain project.

3. Data Mentah: Stores the original raw extraction of retail records containing order volumes, unit prices, product categories, inventory levels, promotions, discounts, weather conditions, and competitor prices.

4. Data Processing: Performs data transformation, cleaning, missing value handling, and calculated field enrichments to prepare clean structured tables for analytical modeling.

5. Pivot: Houses multi dimensional Pivot Tables that aggregate overall revenue, units sold per category, inventory distribution across sales regions, promotional demand impact, and weather condition pricing comparisons.

6. Dashboard: The core executive presentation sheet. It integrates dynamic Pivot Charts, interactive Slicers (Category, Region, Seasonality), automated KPI summary cards driven by helper formulas, and analytical visual cards for final operational decisions.

RESEARCH QUESTIONS ADDRESSED
1. Sales Performance & Stock Levels: How do sales performance (Units Sold & Revenue) and inventory levels differ across product categories and geographic sales regions?
2. Promotion & Discount Impact: How effective are promotions and discounts in driving total volume sold and boosting market demand?
3. External Variables & Competitor Pricing: How do external variables such as weather conditions, seasonality, and competitor price gaps affect product demand fluctuations?
4. Supply Chain & Pricing Optimization: What strategic inventory management and pricing optimization policies should be recommended to prevent lost sales and overstock issues?

KEY FINDINGS AND BUSINESS INSIGHTS
1. Sales Performance & Regional Inventory: Total Revenue reached $155.206.422 with 6.750.976 total units sold. Groceries generated the highest revenue share (> $55M), while the North region recorded the most dominant sales and revenue volume.
2. Promotional Effectiveness: Active promotion campaigns (Promotion = 1) successfully boosted average sales from 84 to 107 units and elevated average market demand from 97 to 123 units per period.
3. Weather & Competitor Pricing Dynamics: Product demand peaks during Sunny weather conditions (Average Demand 115) and drops during Rainy weather (Average Demand 95). Internal pricing remains competitively positioned just below market competitor prices.
4. Inventory Alignment Hotspots: High inventory allocations in North and South regions match high revenue streams, requiring continuous balancing to avoid stockout risks during peak seasonal shifts.

STRATEGIC BUSINESS OUTPUT AND RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Targeted Stock Allocation: Prioritize stock fulfillment and inventory replenishment for high volume categories (Groceries, Furniture) in the North region to minimize lost sales.
2. Weather Dependent Promotions: Align marketing campaigns and promotional discounts with favorable weather windows (Sunny conditions) to maximize conversion rates and demand spikes.
3. Competitive Dynamic Pricing: Maintain competitive price points against market competitors while executing strategic bundling or dynamic discount structures for lower performing categories like Toys.
