EMPLOYEE DEMOGRAPHICS, COMPENSATION & ATTRITION DIAGNOSTIC ANALYSIS

Overview:
This project delivers a diagnostic HR data analysis solution for companies experiencing an increase in employee turnover. By processing raw employee datasets, this project builds a structured Excel workbook featuring automated data preparation, pivot table aggregation, and an interactive executive HR dashboard to evaluate workforce demographics, burnout risks, compensation pay gaps, and department-level attrition rates.

Analyst: Muhammad Ferdy Irmawan, S.Kom.
Dataset Source: Kaggle
Primary Tool: Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning, Formulas, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, KPI Cards)

EXCEL WORKBOOK STRUCTURE AND DETAILED FUNCTIONS
The project file contains multiple sequential worksheets designed to transform raw data into actionable executive insights:

Studi Case: Defines the analytical framework, business background, specific case questions, and expected business outputs for the portfolio project.

Dataset Mentah: Stores the original raw extraction of employee records containing demographics, compensation, job roles, overtime status, and attrition markers.

Data Preparation: Performs data transformation, cleaning, and calculated field enrichments to prepare structured tables for analytical modeling.

Pivot: Houses multi-dimensional Pivot Tables that aggregate employee volume distribution by department/age, calculate average tenure of resigned employees, analyze burnout/overtime risks, and evaluate pay equity across genders and departments.

Dashboard: The core executive presentation sheet. It integrates dynamic Pivot Charts, interactive Slicers (Department, Gender, Attrition Risk Index), automated KPI summary cards driven by formulas, and analytical text boxes for final conclusions.

RESEARCH QUESTIONS ADDRESSED

Demographic Profile & Attrition: What is the demographic profile of employees (age, gender, department) and how is it distributed against the attrition rate?

Burnout & High-Risk Groups: Which employee groups are most vulnerable to experiencing burnout or rapid resignation (short tenure and low satisfaction)?

Compensation Pay Gap: Is there a compensation pay gap across genders or departments within the same job level?

Department Attrition Rate: Which departments or job roles have the highest attrition rate?

KEY FINDINGS AND BUSINESS INSIGHTS

Demographic Profile & Attrition: The overall attrition rate stands at 16.1% (237 out of 1.473 employees), with peak attrition concentrated in younger age groups (18-25 and 26-35 years old).

Burnout & High-Risk Groups: Employees with short tenure (below 3 years) combined with high overtime workloads and low satisfaction trigger the High Risk category.

Compensation & Pay Gap: The company's average compensation is recorded at $6.500,23, and gender/department salary distribution requires continuous monitoring to maintain pay equity.

Departmental Attrition Hotspots: Research & Development and Sales departments record the most dominant turnover volumes.

STRATEGIC BUSINESS OUTPUT AND RECOMMENDATIONS

Targeted High-Risk Intervention: Implement personal approaches, counseling, and engagement initiatives for employees flagged under the High Risk category.

Overtime Policy Evaluation: Review and optimize overtime policies and employee engagement programs to curb burnout among short-tenure staff.

Salary Structure Benchmarking: Regularly adjust and benchmark the salary structure across departments and genders to ensure equitable pay equity and retain top talent.
