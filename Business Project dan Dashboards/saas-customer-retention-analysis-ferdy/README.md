CUSTOMER DEMOGRAPHICS AND RETENTION SEGMENTATION ANALYSIS

Overview:
This project provides data analysis solution for a SaaS company facing a decline in long term customer retention. By processing raw customer datasets, this project builds a structured Excel workbook featuring automated data preparation, pivot table aggregation, and an interactive executive dashboard to segment user demographics and evaluate churn behaviors.

Analyst: Muhammad Ferdy Irmawan, S.Kom.
Dataset Source: Kaggle
Primary Tool: Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning, Formulas, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, KPI Cards)

EXCEL WORKBOOK STRUCTURE AND DETAILED FUNCTIONS
The project file contains multiple sequential worksheets designed to transform raw data into actionable executive insights:
1. Studi Case: Defines the analytical framework, business background, specific case questions, and expected business outputs for the portfolio project.
2. Dataset Mentah: Stores the original raw extraction of SaaS customer records containing unique customer identifiers, gender, age, and subscription tenure.
3. Data Preparation: Performs data transformation and enrichment. It adds categorization logic such as age grouping (age group?) and tenure segmentation (tenure group?) to prepare clean structured tables for analytical modeling.
4. Pivot: Houses multi dimensional Pivot Tables that aggregate customer volume distribution by demographics and calculate average retention/tenure performance across customer segments.
5. Dashboard: The core executive presentation sheet. It integrates dynamic Pivot Charts, interactive Slicers (such as gender and age filters), automated KPI summary cards driven by formulas, and analytical text boxes for final conclusions.

RESEARCH QUESTIONS ADDRESSED
1. Demographic Profile: Who are the primary target customer demographics based on gender and age?
2. Rapid Churn Identification: Which age group experiences the fastest churn (lowest subscription tenure)?
3. Loyalty Mapping: Which segment represents the most loyal long term customers with high tenure?

KEY FINDINGS AND BUSINESS INSIGHTS
1. Primary Demographic Profile: The customer base is predominantly male, with high density concentrations in the >50 years and 25–35 years age brackets.
2. Early Churn Risk: Younger users in the <25 years demographic exhibit the shortest subscription duration, indicating a high propensity for early churn.
3. Core Loyalty Segment: Customers aged >50 years demonstrate the longest average retention period, representing the most stable and loyal user group.

STRATEGIC BUSINESS OUTPUT AND RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Targeted Onboarding Retention Campaign: Implement special promotional discounts or voucher incentives for users aged <25 years during the critical drop off window between day 30 and day 60 of their subscription lifecycle to prevent early churn.
2. Senior Customer Value Protection: Maintain high engagement and customized feature support for the >50 years segment to preserve long term retention rates and stabilize lifetime value.
